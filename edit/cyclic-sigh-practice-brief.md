# Cyclic Sigh render brief

- Composition: one relaxation exercise, one minute, 9:16
- Delivery: 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC
- Voice: OpenRouter `openai/gpt-audio-mini`, Coral, generated as separately timed cues
- Audio: quiet original two-tone bed under spoken guidance
- Visual system: layered pastel sky, softly cut cloud forms, warm horizon glow, frosted phase pills, and subtle analog grain
- Anchor motif: one breathing cloud; it expands on inhale, absorbs a small joining puff on the sip, and releases a drifting cloud trail on exhale
- Shot variation: open-sky center, diagonal drift, then a closer cloud view so the minute develops without changing exercises
- Engagement rhythm: purposeful visual change events land every 4.5 seconds or less, adapted from the OpenMontage anti-slideshow grammar without importing its faster news cadence
- Copy rule: no body-copy slides; screen text is limited to phase cues of four words or fewer
- Loop: sky warmth, cloud drift, and ambient bed complete a sixty-second cycle; the close invites replay and fades cleanly into the opening rest state
- Safety: comfortable, never forced; general wellness practice; stop if uncomfortable
- Evidence: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895
- Paid generation: OpenRouter speech only; rendering and ambient sound are local
