# Cyclic Sigh: one-minute guided practice

**Duration:** 60 seconds  
**Voice:** OpenRouter `openai/gpt-audio-mini`, Coral  
**Purpose:** a calm, optional introduction to one cyclic-sighing practice

| Time | Voice | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00.8 | Let's practice a cyclic sigh. Keep every breath easy, never forced. | CYCLIC SIGH / comfortable, never forced |
| 0:09.6 | Breathe in gently through your nose. | INHALE |
| 0:13.7 | A second, smaller sip. | SMALL SIP |
| 0:16.0 | And a long, unhurried sigh out. | SIGH OUT |
| 0:24.9 | Again. Breathe in. | INHALE |
| 0:29.2 | A smaller sip. | SMALL SIP |
| 0:31.4 | And a long, unhurried sigh out. | SIGH OUT |
| 0:40.3 | One more. Breathe in. | INHALE |
| 0:44.7 | Small sip. | SMALL SIP |
| 0:47.0 | Breathe all the way out, slowly. | SIGH OUT |
| 0:55.0 | Replay when you're ready. Let the rhythm grow familiar. | ONE MORE ROUND? / replay when ready |

## Evidence and safety

- The referenced trial studied daily five-minute practices over one month. This one-minute Reel is a guided introduction, not a claim that one minute reproduces the trial.
- Breaths stay comfortable and are never forced. A viewer can stop and return to normal breathing if uncomfortable.
- Source: Balban et al., *Cell Reports Medicine* (2023), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895
