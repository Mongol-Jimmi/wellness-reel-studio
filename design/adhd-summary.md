# ADHD Design Exploration Summary

## Brief

Explore a consistent, bubbly, approachable, adult brand language for calming mental-wellness Reels. Five isolated frames generated 30 concepts: regulator, game design, biology, ten-year-old, and remove-the-load-bearing-assumption.

## Converged shortlist

1. **Soft Punctuation (`A4`)** — `[N9.5 V9 F9]`, weighted 9.18
2. **Plain-Spoken Pebble (`R4`)** — `[N8.5 V9 F9.5]`, weighted 8.95
3. **Permission Slip (`R1`)** — `[N8 V9 F9]`, weighted 8.65

Contrast prototypes:

- **Inner Weather (`G3`)** — `[N7.5 V9 F9.5]`, weighted 8.60
- **Kind Broadcast (`A6`)** — `[N8 V9 F8.5]`, weighted 8.53

## Traps pruned

- Persistent crisis bands: medicalize routine content and crowd 9:16.
- Arcade gates/meters: retain performance pressure.
- Pinball impacts and unstable houses: risk anxiety-inducing motion.
- Character pebbles: risk infantilizing adults.
- Responsive meshes and stained-glass systems: too costly or contrast-heavy for frequent local production.
- Dense emotional weather maps: too much information for five seconds.

## Non-obvious viable pick

★ **Soft Punctuation** uses ordinary grammatical marks as an ownable motion vocabulary. It avoids wellness clichés while naturally encoding pause, holding, continuation, and landing.

## Wildcard

Could each Reel end with a single punctuation mark that tells the viewer how to carry the prompt forward—comma for continue gently, semicolon for hold two truths, period for stop here?
