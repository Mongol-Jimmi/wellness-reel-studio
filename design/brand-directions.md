# Calming Wellness Brand Directions

## Brief

Create a bubbly, approachable, adult visual system for short-form mental-wellness education. The system should support breathing, sensory grounding, present-moment awareness, and positive coping content without looking clinical, infantilizing the audience, or promising treatment outcomes.

The ADHD exploration produced 30 concepts across five isolated frames. Concepts were scored on novelty (35%), viability (40%), and fit (25%). These five directions form the comparison set.

## 01 — Soft Punctuation (`A4`, 9.18/10)

**Idea:** commas create pauses, parentheses hold thoughts, and periods land gently.

- Palette: oat `#F5EBDD`, ink `#24343A`, coral `#E87762`, sage `#78A88C`, blue `#6C8FB3`
- Motion: drift → hold → gentle landing
- Voice: concise, literate, warm, adult
- Sample: “You can pause, without falling behind.”
- Strength: most ownable and scalable overall
- Risk: excessive bounce would make it childish

## 02 — Plain-Spoken Pebble (`R4`, 8.95/10)

**Idea:** soft asymmetrical tokens carry concrete sensory words such as FEET, ROOM, SOUND, and NOW.

- Palette: oat `#F3EBDD`, charcoal `#303331`, sage, apricot, sky, plum
- Motion: arrive → yield space → breathe together
- Voice: literal, grounded, non-diagnostic
- Sample: “FEET here. SOUND around. NOW.”
- Strength: clearest grounding vocabulary
- Risk: character-like treatment would infantilize adults

## 03 — Permission Slip (`R1`, 8.65/10)

**Idea:** tactile tabs labeled PAUSE, SKIP, and CHOOSE make viewer agency visible.

- Palette: warm paper `#FFF7ED`, ink `#243039`, coral, lilac, mint
- Motion: enter → loop around a phrase → settle
- Voice: optional, autonomy-preserving, never commanding
- Sample: “Pause, if you want. The choice stays yours.”
- Strength: strongest trust and consent language
- Risk: the brand must not imply it grants permission the viewer already owns

## 04 — Inner Weather (`G3`, 8.60/10)

**Idea:** soft weather fields and a neutral dial invite noticing without labeling states good or bad.

- Palette: pale sky `#DCEFF2`, deep teal `#21343B`, sky, sun, lilac, green
- Motion: drift → notice → continue changing
- Voice: observational and nonjudgmental
- Sample: “What’s here—without grading it?”
- Strength: broad emotional-awareness series potential
- Risk: avoid pseudo-clinical emotional meters or fixed diagnoses

## 05 — Kind Broadcast (`A6`, 8.53/10)

**Idea:** a tiny live studio presents each episode as a readable five-second check-in.

- Palette: midnight `#18212B`, cream `#FFF8E8`, yellow, coral, mint, blue
- Motion: lower-third slide → cue light → quiet hold
- Voice: direct, social-native, friendly
- Sample: “Live check-in: notice your shoulders.”
- Strength: strongest episodic packaging and muted-play readability
- Risk: interface feedback must not become scores, streaks, or optimization pressure

## Recommendation

Start with **Soft Punctuation** as the master identity, then borrow **Plain-Spoken Pebble’s concrete sensory vocabulary**. This hybrid is ownable, adult, accessible, easy to render locally, and broad enough for a full content series.

The five clips are visual identity stings—not complete breathing, meditation, or grounding exercises.
