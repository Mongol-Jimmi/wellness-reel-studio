# Mental-Wellness Content Safety Boundary

## Positioning

This brand provides general wellness education and optional coping practices. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or guarantee relief from anxiety, dissociation, derealisation/derealization, panic, or any other condition.

## Evidence-informed wording

- The NHS describes a calming breathing technique for stress, anxiety, and panic: breathe as deeply into the belly as is comfortable **without forcing it**, breathe gently, and repeat for at least five minutes. A five-second brand clip must not be presented as the whole exercise or as an instant reset.<br>
  Source: https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/self-help/guides-tools-and-activities/breathing-exercises-for-stress/
- The U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs describes sensory attention as a grounding practice that can connect attention with the present moment. Use phrases such as “try noticing” or “this may help you reconnect with the present moment,” not “return to reality instantly.”<br>
  Source: https://news.va.gov/104529/live-whole-health-125-grounding-exercise-and-connecting-with-our-senses/
- The NHS defines derealisation as feeling that the world is unreal and notes that assessment, support, and talking therapies may be appropriate for dissociative disorders. Persistent, recurring, frightening, or function-limiting symptoms should be directed toward qualified professional support rather than social-media self-treatment.<br>
  Source: https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/conditions/dissociative-disorders/

## Approved content language

- “Try this gently.”
- “Stop if this feels uncomfortable.”
- “Notice one thing you can see, hear, or feel.”
- “This is a grounding prompt, not medical treatment.”
- “If this keeps happening or feels distressing, consider speaking with a qualified professional.”

## Avoid

- “Cure derealization in five seconds.”
- “Get back to reality instantly.”
- “Reset your nervous system.”
- “Guaranteed anxiety relief.”
- Breath holds, forceful breathing, or medical claims without qualified review.
- Crisis warnings on every routine post; add local, audience-appropriate crisis resources when the actual content warrants them.

## Review gate for future episodes

Every script should record its audience, source, exact claim, intended exercise duration, contraindication/stop language where relevant, and whether qualified clinical review is required. Crisis resources must match the viewer’s country and publishing context.
