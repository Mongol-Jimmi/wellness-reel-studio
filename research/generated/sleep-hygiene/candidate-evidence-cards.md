## TIME • LIGHT • CAFFEINE • ROOM

**Topic:** `sleep-hygiene`

### Easy steps
1. Keep your wake-up time as consistent as you reasonably can.
2. Make the hour before bed dimmer and quieter when possible.
3. Choose one small change tonight instead of trying to fix everything at once.

**Safety boundary:** Sleep habits can support sleep, but persistent sleep problems or major daytime impairment deserve professional assessment.
